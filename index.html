Background

A scientist who builds with data — and now with AI

From clinical medicine to population health intelligence, my work sits at the intersection of evidence and action.

I'm a public health scientist with over a decade of experience leading and supporting evidence-based work that shapes health strategy, strengthens services, and tackles health inequalities. I currently provide population health intelligence and strategic analysis for local government in the UK, alongside advisory and analytical support for organisations in Africa and other settings.

With experience spanning clinical medicine, public health analytics, and programme management, I've worked across highly governed health data environments and routine programme datasets of varying quality. I build reliable multi-year datasets, surface and document data limitations, and strengthen how evidence feeds into reporting, performance monitoring, and population health modelling.

More recently, I've been embedding Generative AI tools — LLMs, agents, and RAG systems — into public health workflows, dramatically cutting the time from data to insight. I believe AI won't replace public health scientists, but scientists who use AI well will change what's possible.
